# Code of conduct

This is a technical project about getting a port right. The people in it are modellers,
programmers and users from many countries and institutions.

- Argue about the matrix, not about the person. Every claim here can be checked against TIMES in
  GAMS; check it, and show the check.
- Be patient with people who are new to Julia, to Python, to TIMES, or to open source. Most of us
  are new to at least one of these.
- Report a defect the way you would want one reported to you: the report file, the model, the
  version, what you expected.
- No harassment, no personal attacks, no discrimination, in issues, pull requests or anywhere
  the project is discussed. Maintainers will remove such content and may block the author.

Concerns go to KanORS-EMR by e-mail, times-os@kanors.com. They will be treated in confidence.
