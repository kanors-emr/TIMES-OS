# Authors

## TIMES

TIMES-OS implements TIMES, the model generator of IEA-ETSAP. The model, its equations and its
documentation are theirs. As listed in the TIMES repository
([etsap-TIMES/TIMES_model](https://github.com/etsap-TIMES/TIMES_model)):

**Original authors:** Ken Noble (Australia), Denise van Regemorter (Belgium), Amit Kanudia (Canada),
Richard Loulou (Canada), Uwe Remme (Germany), Peter Schaumann (Germany), Günter Schmid (Germany),
GianCarlo Tosato (Italy), Tom Kram (Netherlands), Tomas Larsson (Sweden), Gary Goldstein (USA).

**Code owner:** IEA-ETSAP. **Maintainer:** Antti Lehtilä.
